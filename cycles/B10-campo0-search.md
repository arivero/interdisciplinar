## Key Findings

### Official Documents (Primary Sources)

**1. BOE-A-2023-25537 (December 5, 2023)**
- Official resolution establishing Campo 0
- URL: https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2023-25537
- **Status**: Primary legal document, accessed and analyzed

**Campo 0 Definition and Criteria**:
- New field added to recognize clearly interdisciplinary or multidisciplinary research trajectories
- Researchers may submit if work cannot be evaluated from a single disciplinary perspective
- Two pathways:
  1. **Interdisciplinary**: Work applying perspectives, theories, or methods from different disciplines
  2. **Multidisciplinary**: At least two contributions in two different disciplinary fields during evaluation period
- Maximum of two extraordinary contributions allowed
- All contribution types from the eleven disciplinary fields accepted
- Requires justification of interdisciplinary character for each contribution
- Values contributions from funded interdisciplinary research projects
- Positive evaluation requires five contributions with justified relevance and impact from at least two different contribution typologies

## Discussion and Analysis

### What We Learned About Campo 0

1. **Recent Innovation**: Campo 0 was established in December 2023 as part of Spain's research evaluation reform aligned with CoARA commitments and the 2023 University System Law (LOSU).

2. **Rapid Adoption**: 51% growth in applications from first to second year (389 → 587 applications) indicates strong demand for interdisciplinary evaluation pathway.

3. **Broad Disciplinary Coverage**: 138 areas of knowledge represented in 2024 call shows Campo 0 serves researchers across the entire academic spectrum.

4. **Policy vs. Practice Gap**: Molas-Gallart's analysis reveals tension between CoARA's qualitative assessment principles and ANECA's operational reliance on quantifiable metrics (impact factors, etc.).

5. **Structural Barriers**: Spanish legal requirements for "legal certainty," high appeal rates (judicialization), and high-throughput processes (15,000+ applications/year) create pressure for standardized quantitative indicators despite reform intentions.

6. **Two Pathways**: Campo 0 accommodates both:
   - **Interdisciplinarity** (integration across disciplines)
   - **Multidisciplinarity** (parallel work in multiple disciplines)

7. **Evaluation Requirements**: Researchers must actively justify interdisciplinary character and necessity of IDR approach for each contribution.

### Relevance to Our Interdisciplinarity Measurement Project

**Direct Relevance**:
- Campo 0 represents a **real-world implementation** of institutional IDR recognition at national scale
- Provides **empirical data** on interdisciplinary research prevalence (587 applications in 2024)
- Demonstrates **policy need** for IDR measurement frameworks
- Shows **measurement challenges** in practice: how to evaluate contributions that don't fit disciplinary categories

**Methodological Insights**:
- Spain requires both quantitative and qualitative assessment
- Interdisciplinary character must be **justified** (self-reported + evidenced)
- Funding from competitive interdisciplinary calls serves as validation signal
- Requirement for contributions from at least two different typologies acknowledges IDR diversity

**Policy Context**:
- International trend: CoARA, DORA, European research assessment reform
- National implementation faces systemic barriers (legal, bureaucratic, scale)
- Campo 0 shows **institutional demand** for IDR measurement solutions

**Challenges Identified**:
- Quantitative indicators still dominate despite qualitative policy goals
- No consensus on how to measure interdisciplinary character
- Evaluation committees struggle with IDR proposals (wider range of theories/methods)
- Risk of bias against interdisciplinary work persists

### Gaps in Campo 0 Literature

**Limited Academic Analysis**: Despite being a significant policy innovation, very few academic papers analyze Campo 0 itself. Most discussion is in:

**Potential Research Opportunities**:

### Connection to Our Panel Framework

Campo 0's requirement for justification creates opportunity for structured measurement approach:

1. **Our panel components** (diversity, coherence, novelty) could provide systematic framework for characterizing IDR contributions
2. **Two-typology requirement** aligns with our diversity dimension
3. **Justification burden** could be reduced with standardized measurement tools
4. **Evaluation consistency** might improve with quantitative IDR characterization

Campo 0 represents a **use case** for our measurement framework: Spanish researchers need to quantify and justify their interdisciplinary character; our panel could provide evidence.
