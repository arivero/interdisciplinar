# D03 Red Team: Quality Framing Risks

Date: 2026-02-15

## Failure modes

1. **Under-treatment of OQ1**: By declaring quality "orthogonal" to IDR
   measurement, we may appear to dodge the question rather than answer it.
   - Mitigation: The manuscript will explicitly address OQ1 in §6, noting
     how the panel aids quality interpretation. The framing is "quality
     measurement requires IDR characterization" not "quality doesn't matter."

2. **Web search findings may be inaccurate**: The citation penalty / delayed
   impact finding comes from web search summaries, not verified source text.
   - Mitigation: The claim is presented cautiously ("recent meta-evidence
     suggests") and the Cantone 2024 secondary citation provides a verified
     anchor. Full verification deferred to B04.

3. **Xiang et al. 2025 not acquired**: We cite a PNAS finding based on
   web search only.
   - Mitigation: Will cite only the published result (knowledge-base vs.
     topic IDR evaluation outcomes) without interpretive claims. PNAS
     papers are reliably described by their abstracts.
