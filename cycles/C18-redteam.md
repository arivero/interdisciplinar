# C18 Red Team: Translation Repair Risks

Date: 2026-02-15

## Failure modes
- Semantic drift from English source:
  - Mitigation: when in doubt, compare directly to `paper/main.md` and preserve meaning/citations.
- Terminology inconsistency across ES:
  - Mitigation: keep a small glossary of decisions (e.g., `cross-disciplinary`, IKF) and apply consistently.
- Accidental math/content edits:
  - Mitigation: open an `S` cycle for any math/definition doubts; keep `C18` to language-only edits.
