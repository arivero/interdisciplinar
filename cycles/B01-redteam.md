# B01 Red Team: Recency-Focused Bibliography Risks

Date: 2026-02-15

## Failure modes
- Overweighting novelty while missing foundational validity constraints:
  - Mitigation: pair each recent claim with canonical anchor checks.
- Adding many papers without claim-level relevance:
  - Mitigation: require each added source to map to a concrete manuscript claim.
- Recency bias toward trends that may not be robust:
  - Mitigation: prefer studies with transparent methods, replication, or comparative evaluation.
