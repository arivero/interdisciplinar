# S06 Plan: Multi- vs Inter- vs Trans-disciplinarity Distinction

## Motivation
ANECA uses "interdisciplinares y multidisciplinares" in Campo 0 criteria. The main paper must properly address this distinction and its implications for measurement.

## Scope
Study the conceptual distinction between multi-, inter-, and transdisciplinary research as defined in the literature. Map the distinction to indicator properties (Δ, S, E).
