## Executive Summary

This search investigated Spain's national research evaluation framework for interdisciplinary research, focusing on ANECA (Agencia Nacional de Evaluación de la Calidad y Acreditación) and CNEAI (Comisión Nacional Evaluadora de la Actividad Investigadora). Key findings:

1. **Campo 0** (Interdisciplinary and Multidisciplinary Field) was created in CNEAI's 2023 evaluation framework as a dedicated evaluation path for interdisciplinary research
2. Official criteria are published annually in BOE (Boletín Oficial del Estado) resolutions
3. ANECA has joined DORA (2023) and CoARA (2023), committing to responsible research assessment
4. CoARA Spain Chapter published first collective report in September 2025 with interdisciplinarity working group
5. **No explicit academic bibliography** is cited in the official CNEAI/ANECA evaluation criteria documents
6. Spanish bibliometric researchers (Bordons, Morillo, Gómez) have published foundational work on interdisciplinarity measurement

## 2. Campo 0: Interdisciplinary and Multidisciplinary Field

### 2.1 Definition and Purpose

**From official criteria:**

Campo 0 is intended to recognize **clearly interdisciplinary research trajectories**, encompassing contributions that are:
- The result of interdisciplinary research designed or structured by applying perspectives, theories, or methods associated with different disciplines
- Part of multidisciplinary trajectories supported by at least two contributions in two different disciplinary fields during the evaluated period

**When to select Campo 0:**
Researchers should select Campo 0 for evaluation when their research trajectory and contributions have a **marked interdisciplinary character**, reflecting:
- Integration of knowledge, methods, and data from different disciplines
- Synthesis of approaches from multiple fields

### 2.2 Evaluation Requirements

**Justification requirement:**
- For each contribution presented, or for a set of contributions expressing a research trajectory, applicants must **justify the interdisciplinary nature** and the **necessity of using this approach**

**Valued factors:**
- Contributions that are the result of an **interdisciplinary research project awarded in competitive calls** (public or private funding)
- Demonstrated integration across disciplinary boundaries

**Contribution limits:**
- Maximum of **two extraordinary contributions** accepted
- All types of contributions valued in any of the eleven disciplinary fields are permitted

**Positive evaluation threshold:**
- Five contributions of justified relevance and impact that respond to the general criteria described in section I of the resolution

### 2.3 Usage Statistics

**2024 Call (Second year of Campo 0):**
- 587 applications received
- 138 areas of knowledge represented
- 214 applications from civil servants
- 373 applications from employees

**2023 Call (First year of Campo 0):**
- 126 areas of knowledge represented

## 3. ANECA Evaluation Framework Reform

### 3.1 International Commitments

**DORA (San Francisco Declaration on Research Assessment):**
- ANECA joined DORA in **April 2023**
- Commitment: Do not use journal-based metrics as surrogate measure of quality of individual research articles

**CoARA (Coalition for Advancing Research Assessment):**
- ANECA joined CoARA in **April 2023**
- National Chapter Spain approved: **July 2023**
- Official presentation: **February 9, 2024**

**Leiden Manifesto:**
- ANECA adheres to Leiden Manifesto principles
- Commitment: Quantitative measures should support qualitative evaluation
- Metrics should be used responsibly

### 3.2 Evaluation Principles (Post-Reform)

**Key shifts:**
1. Accept **greater diversity of contributions** (beyond journal articles)
2. Move toward evaluation focused on **relevance and impact** rather than dissemination means
3. Promote **open access**
4. Recognize **multidisciplinarity and interdisciplinarity** (Campo 0 creation)
5. Progress toward **more qualitative evaluation** supported by responsible use of quantitative indicators

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Degree of internationalization
- Degree of innovation
- Degree of openness to society
- **Degree of multidisciplinarity or interdisciplinarity**

**References cited in ANECA reform:** None (policy documents do not cite academic papers)

**Methodology:**
- Contributions from **over 60 institutions**
- Four thematic working groups:
  1. Assessment methodologies
  2. Assessment criteria
  3. **Interdisciplinarity**
  4. Awareness and training

### 4.2 Interdisciplinarity Working Group Findings

**Key recommendations:**

1. **Specific training:** Committees should receive specific training on interdisciplinary assessment

2. **Dedicated structures:** Evaluation structures "specifically dedicated to the assessment of interdisciplinary research" should be established

3. **Consensus techniques:** Use of techniques that facilitate consensus among peers from disciplines with different methods, standards, and procedures

4. **Shared trends identified:**
   - Methodological innovations in interdisciplinary assessment
   - Challenges in implementing responsible and context-sensitive assessment practices
   - Need for evaluator training across disciplines

### 9.1 Original "Campo 0" (2010-2022)

**Focus:**
- Company creation
- Patents
- Contracts with socio-economic agents
- Technology transfer activities

**Status:** Replaced in 2023 reform

**Current name:** Interdisciplinary and Multidisciplinary

**Focus:**
- Integration of perspectives, theories, methods from different disciplines
- Multidisciplinary research trajectories
- Clearly interdisciplinary research

**Key difference:** Shift from knowledge transfer to research integration across disciplines

## 13. Key Takeaways

### 13.1 For Interdisciplinary Research Measurement

**ANECA/CNEAI approach:**
- **Qualitative emphasis:** Justification of interdisciplinary necessity required
- **Process-based:** Values interdisciplinary research projects (competitive funding)
- **Integration-focused:** Must demonstrate synthesis, not just juxtaposition
- **Dedicated structure:** Separate evaluation pathway (Campo 0) with specialized committees

**Contrast with bibliometric measurement:**
- Criteria documents do **not specify** bibliometric indicators
- Emphasis on **relevance and impact** over publication venues
- **Peer assessment** of interdisciplinary integration
- Move away from Journal Impact Factor as surrogate for quality

### 13.2 For Spanish Research Context

**National evaluation reform:**
- Creation of Campo 0 is part of broader LOSU (University System Law) implementation
- Alignment with European trends (DORA, CoARA, Leiden Manifesto)
- Over 60 institutions participating in CoARA Spain assessment reform
- Interdisciplinarity is one of four CoARA Spain working groups (alongside methodologies, criteria, training)

**Challenges identified:**
- Need for evaluator training across disciplines
- Consensus-building among peers with different standards
- Balancing qualitative assessment with accountability
- Defining "sufficient" interdisciplinary character

### 13.3 Implications for This Project

**Relevant context:**
1. **National case study:** Spain's Campo 0 is a real-world implementation of interdisciplinary evaluation
2. **Policy without metrics:** Official criteria do not cite specific bibliometric measures
3. **Qualitative turn:** Emphasis on justification and demonstrated integration
4. **European alignment:** Part of broader CoARA movement (60+ institutions in Spain alone)

**Potential citations:**
1. Morillo, Bordons, & Gómez (2001, 2003) - Spanish interdisciplinarity measurement
2. CoARA Spain Chapter report (2025) - Contemporary assessment reform
3. CNEAI as case study of dedicated interdisciplinary evaluation structure

**Contrast with our approach:**
- We develop **quantitative panel** (coherence, diffusion, novelty, diversity)
- ANECA emphasizes **qualitative justification**
- Potential contribution: Our panel could **support** the kind of justification ANECA requires
