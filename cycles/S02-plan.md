# S02 Plan: Evaluation Scenario for National Agency Assessment

Date: 2026-02-15

## Goal
Apply the (Δ, S, E) indicator panel to three mock researcher CVs in a national
evaluation context. Derive a committee composition rule. List failure modes.
Without manuscript edits.

## Deliverable (keep bounded)
- Three mock researcher CVs with realistic publication counts (20–40 papers each).
- Compute (Δ, S, E) for each and show how the panel informs evaluation decisions.
- Committee composition rule derived from the researcher's category profile.
- Failure-mode table with at least 4 modes and mitigations.
- Output: `blackboards/2.md`

## Context from Rafols 2019
- Individual-level indicators have statistical robustness issues.
- Must show multiple dimensions, not aggregate to single score.
- Need contextualization and stakeholder validation.

## Where The Math Goes
- All computation in `blackboards/2.md` only.
- `cycles/S02-*.md` remain concise logs/summaries.

## Allowed files
- `blackboards/`, `notebooks/`, `docs/research-log.gz`, `cycles/S02-*.md`

## Forbidden files
- Manuscripts, `paper/notes/`, bibliography ledger, `sources/`

## Acceptance criteria
- Three mock CVs evaluated with explicit panel values.
- Committee composition rule stated as a formal procedure.
- Failure-mode table with ≥ 4 entries.
- Rafols 2019 cautions explicitly addressed.
- Discovery flag filled.
