Date: 2026-02-15

## Goal
Design a structured taxonomy of interdisciplinarity indicators and plan
the expansion of paper/main.md into a genuine area review. The manuscript
currently reads as a single-result methods paper; it needs review sections
that survey the landscape.

## Context

- Indicator map/taxonomy table (FG1)

## Approach
1. Study acquired sources (especially Cantone 2024 comprehensive review,
   Wang & Schneider 2020, Zhou et al. 2023) to identify indicator families.
2. Design a taxonomy structure that organizes indicators by dimension
   (diversity, coherence, impact) and methodology (reference-based,
   citation-based, text-based, network-based).
3. Propose a new manuscript outline with review sections + our original
   contribution as the core.

## Success criteria
- Taxonomy table covering at least 4 indicator families.
- Proposed manuscript outline with section headers.
- Clear mapping of acquired sources to review sections.
