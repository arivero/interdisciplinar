# D04 Plan: Institutional Measurement Literature

Date: 2026-02-15

## Goal
Investigate what the literature says about institutional-level measurement
of interdisciplinarity — which indicators can be computed with internal
university data, and what institutional measurement looks like in practice.
This addresses OQ2 and informs Satellite 1 scope.

## Context
The manuscript's §5.1 (Institutional Self-Assessment) currently gives a
brief sketch (~8 lines). OQ2 asks which concrete IDR measurements can be
done with full institutional data access. Satellite 1 is planned as a short
letter on this topic.

## Approach
1. Search acquired sources for institutional measurement discussion.
2. Web search for recent work on university-level IDR measurement.
3. Map our panel components to institutional data availability.
4. Identify what data gaps remain for institutional deployment.

## Success criteria
- Clear mapping of (Δ, S, E) to institutional data requirements.
- List of additional indicators computable with internal data that
  bibliometric sources cannot support.
- Assessment of whether §5.1 needs expansion or whether this is purely
  Satellite 1 territory.
