# D01 Red Team: Discovery Risks for Satellite 2

Date: 2026-02-15

## Failure modes
- "Novelty" is only renaming existing evaluation advice:
  - Mitigation: B02 scan for existing agency protocols (REF, ERA, ANECA, ERC). If protocols exist, differentiate explicitly or abandon.
- Indicator panel doesn't scale to individual careers:
  - Mitigation: S02 witness must use realistic publication counts (10–50 papers, not 5), and test with sparse citation data.
- Paper becomes normative without empirical grounding:
  - Mitigation: keep Satellite 2 tightly coupled to computable indicators; avoid broad policy speculation.
- Conflation of "interdisciplinar" classification with genuine integration:
  - Mitigation: include the specialist-misclassified-as-interdisciplinary case in the S02 witness to show the panel catches this.
