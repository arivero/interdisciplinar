# S04 Debate: Institutional Measurement Design

Date: 2026-02-15

## Should grant diversity be an institutional indicator?

**For:** Grant panel assignments are institutional data (finance/research
office records). Funders assign proposals to review panels, and cross-panel
funding suggests interdisciplinary capacity.

**Against:** Grant success depends on many factors beyond interdisciplinarity
(seniority, network, writing skill). A specialist might win grants from
multiple panels simply by submitting broadly.

**Resolution:** Include grant diversity as a *supplementary* indicator but
weight it below co-authorship and co-supervision diversity. Grants show
capacity to pitch across fields; collaborations show actual integration.

## Should we include teaching data?

**For:** Cross-departmental teaching (e.g., physicist teaching in engineering
program) is a form of knowledge integration visible only to the institution.

**Against:** Teaching assignments often reflect administrative need, not
research interdisciplinarity. A specialist might teach outside their
department for workload reasons.

**Resolution:** Omit teaching from Satellite 1 scope. Focus on research
indicators. Teaching-based IDR is a separate topic.

## Three researchers sufficient for witness?

**For:** The scenario demonstrates the key insight (institutional data reveals
polymathy vs. integration). Three archetypes (integrator, polymath, specialist)
suffice.

**Against:** Real departments have 10-50 researchers. Three is too small to
show distributional patterns.

**Resolution:** Three is sufficient for a blackboard witness. Satellite 1
can note "This scenario uses 3 researchers for illustration; institutional
deployment should compute indicators for all active researchers and examine
distributions."
