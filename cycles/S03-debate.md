# S03 Debate: Clean Proportions vs Consistent Data

Date: 2026-02-15

## Question
Should we redesign publication vectors to get round proportions, or keep
existing vectors with non-round proportions?

## Current position
Keep existing publication vectors. The proportions (0.300, 0.075, 0.175, 0.350, 0.100)
are not round but are internally consistent. Non-round proportions actually look more
realistic and rigorous than hand-picked round numbers. The discrimination story is
preserved (gap 0.021 is still small vs range 0-1).
