## One-sentence novelty claim (non-manuscript)
The (diversity, coherence, cross-field effect) indicator panel, combined with a category-profile-driven committee composition rule and an explicit failure-mode taxonomy, provides a structured evaluation protocol for national agencies assessing researchers in the "Interdisciplinar" science group.

## Scope disclaimer (non-manuscript)
This claim is bounded to the evaluation of individual researchers who self-classify or are classified as interdisciplinary. It does not address departmental or institutional evaluation, nor does it prescribe funding allocation. The protocol assumes bibliometric and project data availability comparable to what national agencies typically collect.
