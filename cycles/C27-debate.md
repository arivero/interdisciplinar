# C27 Debate: How comprehensive should the cheatsheet be?

Date: 2026-02-15

## Question
Should the cheatsheet list every display-mode formula even when expressions repeat later in the manuscript, or should repeated forms be consolidated?

## Options
1. List each display formula as it appears, including repeated forms in later sections.
2. Consolidate identical expressions into a single entry with cross-references.

## Decision
Option 2: consolidate identical expressions into one entry while noting the sections where they appear.

## Rationale
Consolidation keeps the cheatsheet compact and readable while still covering all defined formulas; section notes preserve traceability to manuscript locations.

## Consequences / Follow-ups
- Cheatsheet entries will cite the sections where each formula occurs to avoid ambiguity.
