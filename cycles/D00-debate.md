# D00 Debate: Single Composite Metric vs Indicator Panel

Date: 2026-02-15

## Question
Should the project pursue one composite interdisciplinarity score or a structured panel
of complementary indicators?

## Decision
Default to an indicator panel.

## Rationale
Rafols-based evidence emphasizes multidimensionality and level dependence; a single
score risks masking tradeoffs between diversity, coherence, and observed cross-field effect.
