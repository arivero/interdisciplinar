# B11 Debate: Ledger vs. Manuscript Reference Lists

Date: 2026-02-15

## Question
Should we require *every* reference-list entry in `paper/main*.md` to have a ledger key in
`paper/bibliography.md`, even for policy reports/books without DOIs?

## Current resolution
Yes for any work that is cited in the manuscript body. For non-DOI items, use `PENDING_LIBRARY`
with a human-readable acquisition note.
