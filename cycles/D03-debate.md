# D03 Debate: Quality Dimension Design Choices

Date: 2026-02-15

## Should quality be a panel component?

**For**: OQ1 explicitly asks about quality measurement. A quality indicator
(e.g., normalized citation count) would make the panel self-contained.

**Against**: Quality measurement is a general bibliometric problem, not
specific to IDR. Adding a citation-based quality component to an IDR panel
conflates two orthogonal concerns. Moreover, the panel is designed to
characterize the *type* of interdisciplinarity, not to assess whether the
research is good.

**Resolution**: Quality stays outside the panel. The manuscript should
discuss how the panel *aids interpretation* of quality indicators (by
revealing IDR type) rather than trying to subsume quality into the IDR
measurement.
