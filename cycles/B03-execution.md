## Key findings for the review
- Zwanenburg et al. 2022 extends Wang & Schneider — evaluates 21 measures systematically
- Nakhoda et al. 2023 shows RS index has significant uncertainty via bootstrapping
- Cantone 2024 comprehensive recent review of measurement design choices
- Xiang et al. 2025 (PNAS!) distinguishes topic-IDR from knowledge-base-IDR with opposite peer review effects
- Cantone 2025 shows LLMs can estimate disciplinary similarity comparably to citational methods — future direction for our s_{ij} matrix
- Self-reported vs bibliometric 2026: individual-level correlations only 0.13-0.18 — supports our point that single indicators are insufficient
