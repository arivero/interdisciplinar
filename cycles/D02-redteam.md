# D02 Red Team: Taxonomy and Structure Risks

Date: 2026-02-15

## Failure modes

1. **Taxonomy may be incomplete** — we relied on three post-2020 reviews
   plus our own reading. An indicator family may exist that none of our
   sources covers (e.g., patent-based or funding-based measures).
   - Mitigation: The taxonomy is presented as "indicators identified in the
     bibliometric literature" not as exhaustive. The 5 PENDING sources may
     add entries when acquired.

2. **Wrap-around structure may create coherence gap** — existing §2–4 were
   written as a standalone methods paper. When embedded in §4 of a larger
   review, the tone/level may feel discontinuous.
   - Mitigation: C02 content cycle should smooth transitions. A brief
     bridge paragraph at end of §3 ("Against this backdrop, we now present
     our panel...") and start of §5 ("Having demonstrated...") should suffice.

3. **Novelty dimension treatment too thin** — a single paragraph may seem
   dismissive given Cantone's emphasis.
   - Mitigation: Acceptable for a review focused on operational measurement.
     Novelty indicators lack the empirical validation of diversity/coherence
     measures. We are transparent about the scope boundary.

4. **Zhou's IKF not yet published in final form** — the acquired source is
   an arXiv preprint (2207.10722). JASIST publication status unconfirmed.
   - Mitigation: Cite as "Zhou et al. (2023, preprint)" until confirmed.
     The framework is methodologically sound regardless.

5. **5 PENDING sources may reshape the taxonomy** — especially Xiang et al.
   2025 (PNAS) and Zwanenburg et al. 2022 (consistency evaluation).
   - Mitigation: The taxonomy structure (4 dimensions × 4 methods) is robust
     enough to absorb new entries. New indicators slot into existing cells.
