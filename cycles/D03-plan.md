# D03 Plan: Quality Dimension Framing

Date: 2026-02-15

## Goal
Investigate how the literature frames "quality" of interdisciplinary research
(OQ1 from motivations.md). The manuscript currently addresses impact through
the cross-field effect E but doesn't discuss quality measurement challenges
specific to IDR.

## Context
OQ1 asks: "Can we reliably measure the impact and quality of interdisciplinary
researchers? What definitions of 'impact' and 'quality' remain valid across
disciplinary boundaries and over realistic time horizons?"

The manuscript's panel addresses *impact* (via E) but not *quality* per se.
Key questions:
1. Is "quality" even separable from "impact" in bibliometric measurement?
2. Does IDR face specific quality measurement biases (e.g., citation penalties)?
3. Should the panel include a quality component, or is quality orthogonal?

## Approach
1. Search acquired sources for claims about IDR quality, citation penalties,
   and impact measurement challenges.
2. Check if any PENDING sources (especially Xiang et al. 2025 PNAS:
   "Evaluating interdisciplinary research: Disparate outcomes") address this.
3. Design a framing for how "quality" relates to our taxonomy and panel.

## Success criteria
- Clear articulation of how quality relates to the four taxonomy dimensions.
- Decision on whether the manuscript needs a quality discussion or whether
  E (diffusion) sufficiently addresses OQ1.
