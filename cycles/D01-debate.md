# D01 Debate: Is This a Methods Paper or a Policy Paper?

Date: 2026-02-15

## Question
Should Satellite 2 focus on the technical indicator-panel methodology (how to compute
indicators for an individual researcher) or on the policy recommendations (committee
composition, evaluation protocol design)?

## Current position
Both — but weighted toward methodology with concrete recommendations. The indicator
panel provides the technical backbone; the committee composition and failure-mode
taxonomy make it actionable for agencies. Without the methodology, it's just opinion;
without the policy framing, it's just metrics.

## What would change my mind?
- If existing agency guidelines already specify indicator-based protocols for
  interdisciplinary researchers, our contribution shrinks to "use better indicators."
  A B02 literature scan would reveal this.
- If the (Δ, S, E) panel does not work at the individual-career level (e.g., because
  citation data is too sparse for early-career researchers), the methodology claim fails
  and only the policy framing survives.
