This search identified **23 key academic sources** cited by CoARA and related research assessment reform initiatives on interdisciplinarity. We successfully acquired **2 papers** via sci-hub. ## Key Findings and Connections

### 1. Rafols Network

**Ismael Rafols** is a central figure connecting:
- Leiden Manifesto (2015) - co-author
- CoARA Spain working group on interdisciplinarity
- Multiple bibliometric papers on IDR measurement (Porter & Rafols 2009, Rafols & Meyer 2010, Leydesdorff & Rafols 2011)
- Oxford Handbook chapter with Huutoniemi (2017)
- Wagner et al. review (2011)
- Molas-Gallart impact study (2014)

### 2. Assessment Reform Lineage

DORA (2012) → Leiden Manifesto (2015) → Metric Tide (2015) → EC Scoping Report (2021) → CoARA Agreement (2022)

Each builds on the previous, with increasing sophistication on interdisciplinarity.

### 3. Methodological Evolution

- Early work (Stirling 2007, Porter & Rafols 2009): Diversity indices
- Middle period (Rafols & Meyer 2010): Diversity + coherence
- Recent (Laursen et al. 2022): Meta-analysis of 142 assessment approaches
- Current (CoARA 2022-2025): Implementation in institutional practice

### 4. Gaps Identified

CoARA Spain references show areas we haven't fully explored:
- **Leinster-Cobbold diversity indices** (Mugabushaka et al. 2016)
- **Interdisciplinarity and collaboration** relationship (Zhang et al. 2018)
- **Career impacts** of IDR (Vantard et al. 2023)
- **Validation studies** linking bibliometrics to lab practices (Roessner et al. 2013)

## Recommendations

1. **Cite Wagner et al. (2011)** in main paper - comprehensive review of IDR measurement approaches
2. **Integrate Molas-Gallart et al. (2014)** - addresses IDR-impact relationship, shows IDR is neither necessary nor sufficient for impact
3. **Acquire Laursen et al. (2022)** by other means - most comprehensive systematic review available
4. **Download open access policy documents** (EC 2021, Metric Tide 2015, LERU 2023) for policy context
5. **Consider expanding bibliography** with papers on diversity indices (Leinster-Cobbold approach)
6. **Integrate National Academies (2005)** - currently orphan in our bibliography
