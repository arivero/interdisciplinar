1. **Lagar-Barbosa, M.P., Escalona-Fernández, M.I., & Pulgarín, A. (2014)**. "Análisis de la interdisciplinariedad en la ingeniería química universitaria española". *Revista Española de Documentación Científica*, 37(1), e029.
   - DOI: 10.3989/redc.2014.1.1048
   - **File**: `/Users/arivero/interdisc/sources/lagar-2014-chemical-engineering.md`
   - **Content**: Spanish case study analyzing interdisciplinarity in chemical engineering using JCR category co-assignment. Found 598 journals distributed among 115 categories, with 54% of journals and 56% of documents assigned to multiple categories. Used VOS (Visualization of Similarities) technique to map category co-occurrence, identifying 13 clusters. Most central categories: "Engineering, Chemical", "Chemistry, Physical", and "Biotechnology & Applied Microbiology".

2. **Torres-Salinas, D., & Jiménez-Contreras, E. (2012)**. "Hacia las unidades de bibliometría en las universidades: modelo y funciones". *Revista Española de Documentación Científica*, 35(3), 469-480.
   - DOI: 10.3989/redc.2012.3.959
   - **File**: `/Users/arivero/interdisc/sources/torres-salinas-2012-bibliometric-units.md`
   - **Content**: Proposes model for bibliometric units in Spanish universities based on three pillars: (1) control of research information sources, (2) preparing reports and analyses, (3) advice/training. Presents examples from Universidad de Navarra and Universidad de Granada. Contextualizes growth of bibliometric evaluation in Spanish universities facing competitive pressures.

3. **Molas-Gallart, J., Rafols, I., & Tang, P. (2015)**. "On the relationship between interdisciplinarity and impact: different modalities of interdisciplinarity lead to different types of impact". *Journal of Science Policy and Research Management*, 29(2), 69-89.
   - arXiv: 1412.6684
   - **File**: `/Users/arivero/interdisc/sources/molas-gallart-2015-interdisciplinarity-impact.md`
   - **Content**: Empirical study of 15 UK ESRC Social Sciences research investments examining relationship between interdisciplinarity and societal impact. Key finding: interdisciplinarity is neither necessary nor sufficient for societal impact, but "long-range" interdisciplinarity (problem-oriented, strong stakeholder engagement) more likely associated with societal impact. Molas-Gallart from INGENIO (CSIC-UPV), Rafols originally from Barcelona.

9. **ANECA/CNEAI Campo 0 (Interdisciplinary Field)** - 2024-2025 evaluation criteria
   - BOE-A-2024-26587 (9 December 2024) and BOE resolution 10 December 2025
   - **Content**: New interdisciplinary evaluation field. Definitions:

    - All contributions evaluated for: internationalization, innovation, openness to society, multidisciplinarity/interdisciplinarity

## Key Findings About Spanish Research on Interdisciplinarity

### Historical Contributions

1. **Pioneering bibliometric methods** (2001-2004): Morillo, Bordons & Gómez at CSIC developed foundational bibliometric indicators for interdisciplinarity:
   - ISI multi-classification of journals
   - Cross-category citation/reference patterns
   - Chemical Abstracts multi-assignation
   - Typology of disciplines by degree of interdisciplinarity

2. **Empirical studies**: Early 2000s surveys of Spanish research teams (cardiovascular 2002, chemical engineering 2014) combining bibliometrics with researcher surveys - three-dimensional approach:
   - Diversity in training/specialization
   - Research practices (cross-disciplinary knowledge/collaboration)
   - Cognitive inputs/outputs (journal diversity)

### Recent Developments (2020s)

3. **Policy reform era**: Spain undergoing major research evaluation reform:
   - CoARA National Chapter Spain (2024): 84 institutions participating
   - ANECA Campo 0 creation (2024-2025): dedicated interdisciplinary evaluation field
   - Clear conceptual distinction: interdisciplinarity (research-level) vs. multidisciplinarity (trajectory-level)

4. **Institutional infrastructure**: Spanish universities establishing bibliometric units (Torres-Salinas & Jiménez-Contreras 2012 model):
   - Universidad de Granada, Universidad de Navarra examples
   - Three-pillar model: information control, reporting, training/advice

5. **Conceptual advances**: Spanish-connected researchers (Molas-Gallart, Rafols) contributing to understanding interdisciplinarity-impact relationship:
   - Different modalities of interdisciplinarity lead to different impact types
   - "Long-range" interdisciplinarity more associated with societal impact
   - Problem-orientation and stakeholder engagement key factors

### Research Ecosystem Characteristics

6. **Strong CSIC presence**: Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) central to bibliometric research:
   - IPP institute houses core researchers (Bordons, Morillo, Gómez)
   - Joint institutes with universities (INGENIO with UPV)
   - ÍnDICEs-CSIC infrastructure for Spanish journals

7. **University-based groups**: Three major academic centers:
   - **Granada (EC3)**: Largest Spanish bibliometric group, tool development, altmetrics
   - **Carlos III Madrid (LEMI)**: Associated with CSIC, philosophical foundations
   - **Valencia (INGENIO)**: Policy-oriented, evaluation methods, transformative innovation

8. **International connections**: Spanish researchers well-integrated internationally:
   - Rafols: Barcelona → Leiden → Sussex
   - Molas-Gallart: UPV-CSIC ↔ Sussex
   - Publications in international journals, participation in UK/EU projects

### Spanish-Language Contributions

9. **REDC as publication venue**: *Revista Española de Documentación Científica* (CSIC) is main Spanish-language outlet:
   - Quarterly since 1977, 4 issues/year
   - Open access to many articles
   - Publishes in Spanish on scientometrics, webometrics, altmetrics, research evaluation
   - ~34-39 documents/year (2015-2024)

10. **Practical focus**: Spanish research often addresses evaluation system challenges:
    - ANECA/CNEAI evaluation criteria
    - Sexenios (six-year research productivity assessment)
    - University rankings and institutional evaluation
    - Differences across disciplines in Spanish science
