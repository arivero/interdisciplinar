## Key Findings
- Multi = high Δ, low S (diversity without integration)
- Inter = moderate-high Δ, high S (diversity with integration)
- Trans = variable Δ, high S + non-academic actors (not bibliometrically capturable)
- Single-index measures (Rao-Stirling alone) conflate multi with inter
- The panel (Δ, S, E) can disambiguate modes
