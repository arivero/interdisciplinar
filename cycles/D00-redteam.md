# D00 Red Team: Discovery Failure Modes

Date: 2026-02-15

## Failure modes
- Novelty target is only repackaging known claims:
  - Mitigation: require an explicit falsifiable claim and a new witness design tied to internal university data.
- Scope creep into manuscript drafting:
  - Mitigation: keep D00 restricted to claim selection and spawn specification.
- Ambiguous definitions of impact/quality:
  - Mitigation: force operational definitions before any promotion cycle.
