# S02: National Agency Evaluation Scenario

Ref: S00 (`blackboards/0.md`), S01 (`blackboards/1.md`), Rafols 2019

## Setup

National evaluation agency assesses three researchers classified as "Interdisciplinar."

### Category system (same as S00)

| ID | Label |
|----|-------|
| C1 | Physics, condensed matter |
| C2 | Materials science |
| C3 | Chemistry, physical |
| C4 | Biology, molecular |
| C5 | Mathematics, applied |

## Mock Researcher CVs

### Dr. A — Genuine cross-disciplinary integrator

- 30 publications over 12 years
- Category distribution p_A = (0.30, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.10)
- Publications bridge C1–C4 with shared methodology
- Cited by articles in 4 of 5 categories
- Panel: **Δ = 0.558, S = 0.733, E = 0.600**

### Dr. B — Polymath (broad but disconnected)

- 35 publications over 15 years
- Category distribution p_B = (0.25, 0.20, 0.20, 0.20, 0.15)
- Publications are single-field contributions in separate domains
- Cited almost exclusively within each publication's own field
- Panel: **Δ = 0.562, S = 0.000, E = 0.063**

### Dr. D — Misclassified specialist

- 25 publications over 10 years
- Category distribution p_D = (0.60, 0.25, 0.10, 0.00, 0.05)
- Publications cluster tightly in C1+C2 (neighboring fields)
- Cited primarily within condensed matter community
- Panel: **Δ = 0.288, S = 0.881, E = 0.211**

## Panel Interpretation for Evaluation

### Decision matrix

| Researcher | Δ | S | E | Evaluation signal |
|------------|-------|-------|-------|-------------------|
| Dr. A | 0.558 | 0.733 | 0.600 | Genuine integrator: broad + coherent + cross-field impact |
| Dr. B | 0.562 | 0.000 | 0.063 | Polymath: broad but no integration, no cross-field impact |
| Dr. D | 0.288 | 0.881 | 0.211 | Misclassified: narrow specialist, should be reclassified |

### Evaluation recommendations

- **Dr. A**: qualifies for "Interdisciplinar" assessment; committee must span C1 and C4
- **Dr. B**: does not qualify as interdisciplinary researcher despite breadth; recommend reassignment to strongest single field or split evaluation across fields
- **Dr. D**: misclassified; should be evaluated as C1 specialist by standard disciplinary panel

## Committee Composition Rule

**Procedure.** Given researcher r with category proportion vector p_r:

1. Identify **primary categories**: K_r = {i : p_{r,i} ≥ τ}, where τ = 0.15
2. For each category i ∈ K_r, include ≥ 1 evaluator from discipline i
3. Include ≥ 1 evaluator with demonstrated cross-disciplinary expertise (Δ > 0.4)
4. Committee size: |K_r| + 1 (disciplines + cross-disciplinary chair)

### Application

| Researcher | K_r | Committee disciplines | Size |
|------------|-----|----------------------|------|
| Dr. A | {C1, C3, C4} | CM physics, phys. chem., mol. biology + IDR chair | 4 |
| Dr. B | {C1, C2, C3, C4} | All four + IDR chair (but evaluation will reveal non-integration) | 5 |
| Dr. D | {C1, C2} | CM physics, materials science + IDR chair | 3 |

## Failure-Mode Taxonomy

| # | Mode | Description | Mitigation |
|---|------|-------------|------------|
| F1 | Breadth-without-depth reward | High Δ rewarded regardless of S and E | Require S > threshold and E > threshold for "integrator" classification |
| F2 | Non-standard publication penalty | Interdisciplinary journals have lower impact factors | Use field-normalized citation indicators; do not compare IF across fields |
| F3 | Incommensurable impact across fields | Citation norms differ 10x between fields (math vs biology) | Normalize E by field-specific citation baselines |
| F4 | Misclassification persistence | Specialist enters "Interdisciplinar" and stays there | Panel-based reclassification trigger: if Δ < 0.35, recommend reclassification |
| F5 | Early-career data sparsity | Junior researchers have too few publications for reliable panel values | Minimum publication threshold (e.g., n ≥ 15); use confidence intervals below threshold |
| F6 | Gaming via strategic co-authorship | Researcher adds co-authors from distant fields to inflate Δ | Weight Δ by corresponding-author publications only; cross-check S for coherence |

## Rafols 2019 Cautions (Addressed)

| Caution | How addressed |
|---------|---------------|
| Statistical non-robustness at individual level | F5: minimum publication threshold; confidence intervals |
| Show multiple dimensions, not single score | Panel presented as triple (Δ, S, E), never aggregated |
| Contextualization needed | Committee composition derived from researcher's profile |
| Stakeholder validation required | Protocol requires agency adaptation; not one-size-fits-all |
| Performative harm / gaming | F6: corresponding-author weighting; S cross-check |
