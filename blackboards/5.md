# S06: Multi- vs Inter- vs Trans-disciplinarity Distinction

Ref: ANECA "interdisciplinares y multidisciplinares"; OECD 1998; Wagner et al. 2011; Morillo et al. 2003; Aksnes et al. 2026; Molas-Gallart et al. 2014

## OECD Tripartite Typology (1998, via Morillo et al. 2003)

| Mode | Definition | Integration level |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Multi** | Different disciplinary perspectives; NO integration | Sum of parts |
| **Inter** | Theoretical/conceptual/methodological synthesis | Greater than sum |
| **Trans** | Mutual integration of epistemologies; transcends boundaries | New framework |

Source: Morillo, Bordons, Gómez (2003, JASIST 54:1237): "multidisciplinary research, the subject under study is approached from different angles, using different disciplinary perspectives and integration is not accomplished. Interdisciplinary research leads to the creation of a theoretical, conceptual, and methodological identity."

## Wagner et al. 2011 (Table 1) — Refined Definitions

- **Multi**: "juxtapose disciplinary/professional perspectives… no more and no less than the simple sum of its parts"
- **Inter**: "integrate separate disciplinary data, methods, tools, concepts, and theories… different from, and greater than, the sum of its parts"
  - Indicators: micro-combinations of models, hybrid interlanguage, altered perspectives
- **Trans**: "comprehensive frameworks that transcend the narrow scope of disciplinary worldviews"
- **Cross-disciplinary** (Aksnes 2026, Hammarfelt 2020): referencing literature from another field without integration

## Mapping to Indicator Components

| Mode | Δ (diversity) | S (coherence) | E (cross-field effect) |
|------|--------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Multi | High | Low | Low-moderate |
| Inter | Moderate-high | High | High |
| Trans | Variable | High | High + non-academic |
| Specialist | Low | High | Low |

**Key insight** (Rafols & Meyer 2009, via Wagner 2011 §5.1):
- "disciplinary diversity indicates the large-scale breadth of the knowledge base (the multidisciplinary scope)"
- "changes from lower to higher network coherence reflect knowledge integration"
- Therefore: Δ alone captures multi; S distinguishes inter from multi; E captures cross-field impact

## Empirical Evidence: Multi ≠ Inter

Aksnes et al. 2026 survey (N=1,498 papers, self-rated):
- 42% rated as BOTH multidisciplinary AND interdisciplinary
- 23% rated as PARTLY both
- Multi and inter are NOT mutually exclusive in practice
- Bibliometric measures (Rao-Stirling) rarely correlate with self-reported IDR type

## Molas-Gallart IDR Modalities (2014, arXiv)

Two contrasting modalities (via Boschma 2005 proximity framework):

| Dimension | Long-range IDR | Short-range IDR |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Cognitive distance | SSH-STEM (long) | Within SSH (short) |
| Knowledge integration | Integrative | Multi-perspective |
| Practice | Joint teams | Specialized teams |
| Stakeholders | Collaborative | Transfer |
| Impact type | Direct use | Information |

## ANECA Implications

ANECA distinguishes "interdisciplinares y multidisciplinares" in Campo 0.
- This implies evaluation criteria should differ by mode
- Multi researcher: high Δ, committee needs breadth coverage
- Inter researcher: high S, committee needs integration expertise
- Panel (Δ, S, E) can diagnose which mode applies

## Directionality: Input/Output Flow Patterns

When the output of one area is used as input in another:

| Pattern | Name in literature | Example | Integration? |
|---------|-------------------|---------|--------------|
| A→B one-way, no synthesis | **Sequential multidisciplinarity** (Stokols 2003, via Abramo 2018) | Lab uses statistical software | No |
| A→B one-way, transforms B | **Instrumental/auxiliary interdisciplinarity** (Klein 1996, 2008) | ML methods transform genomics | Partial |
| A↔B bidirectional exchange | **Reciprocal interdisciplinarity** | Physics ↔ mathematics | Yes |
| A→B measured by citations | **Interdisciplinary Knowledge Flow (IKF)** (Zhou, Guns & Engels 2023) | PCTC metric | Measured |

Key citations:
- Abramo, D'Angelo, Zhang 2018: "Multidisciplinary: researchers work independently and sequentially"
- Klein 2008 (via Abramo): each type "characterized by a particular type of knowledge integration"
- Zhou, Guns, Engels 2023: "disciplines import and export knowledge from/to other disciplines" — three aspects: broadness, intensity, homogeneity
- Choi & Pak 2006: multi/inter/trans = "continuum of increasing levels of involvement"

### Panel mapping for flow patterns
- Sequential multi: high Δ_ref BUT in segregated clusters → low S
- Instrumental inter: moderate Δ_ref with directional coherence → moderate S
- Reciprocal inter: high Δ_ref with bidirectional coherence → high S

## Measurement Gap

- **Multi**: well-captured by Rao-Stirling Δ, Shannon H, variety N
- **Inter**: requires coherence S (Rafols-Meyer network density) + integration evidence
- **Trans**: NO standard bibliometric indicator; requires qualitative/survey data
- Implication: single-index measures (Rao-Stirling alone) conflate multi with inter

## S07: ANECA Sexenio Campo 0 — Official Criteria

### Legal Basis
- Ley Orgánica 2/2023, Art. 11.7: "en todos los campos se valorarán positivamente los resultados de investigaciones multidisciplinares e interdisciplinares"
- Campo 0 created in 2023 resolution (BOE-A-2023-25537)
- Updated annually: BOE-A-2024-26587, BOE-A-2025-26118

### Campo 0 Title: "Interdisciplinar y multidisciplinar"
- **Transdisciplinar: NOT mentioned** in any version (2023, 2024, 2025)

### Two Modes Defined by ANECA — INPUT vs OUTPUT Distinction

| Mode | ANECA definition | Direction | Panel mapping |
|------|-----------------|-----------|---------------|
| **Interdisciplinar** | "diseñadas o estructuradas aplicando perspectivas, teorías o métodos asociados con diferentes disciplinas" | **INPUT** (how research is designed) | High Δ_ref (reference diversity), high S |
| **Multidisciplinar** | "trayectorias respaldadas con al menos dos aportaciones en campos disciplinares diferentes" | **OUTPUT** (where results land) | High variety across publication fields |

Cf. Wagner et al. 2011, fn. 10: "common usage of multi-, inter-, and trans-disciplinary rarely distinguish between the input and output directions of IDR"
→ ANECA **does** distinguish by direction: inter = input-side, multi = output-side

### Evaluation Requirements
- 5 contributions of justified relevance and impact
- At least 2 different contribution typologies (from 11 disciplinary fields)
- Must justify interdisciplinary character for each contribution
- Projects with competitive funding valued positively

### Key Observation
ANECA's inter/multi distinction tracks INPUT vs OUTPUT:
- **Inter** = research designed using multiple disciplines' perspectives (input → Δ_ref + S)
- **Multi** = trajectory spanning multiple disciplinary fields (output → publication diversity)
- **Trans** = absent from criteria (not bibliometrically measurable)
- A researcher can be inter but not multi (integrative methods, publishes in one field)
- A researcher can be multi but not inter (publishes in many fields without integration)

### Implications for Paper
1. §5 (Practice) or §6 (Open Problems): document ANECA Campo 0 as concrete policy implementation
2. Note that the legal framework ALREADY distinguishes inter from multi
3. The panel (Δ, S, E) operationalizes exactly what ANECA requires but cannot yet measure
4. Trans gap: ANECA avoids it — consistent with bibliometric limitations
5. The 5-contribution minimum + 2-typology rule is itself a crude diversity check (variety ≥ 2)
