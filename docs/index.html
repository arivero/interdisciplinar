Measuring Interdisciplinarity with Internal University Data: An Operational Guide
A. Rivero and A.I. Scaffold | 2026
Universities possess rich internal data that external bibliometric services cannot access. We present an operational measurement protocol that leverages institutional data to assess interdisciplinary research at the departmental and institutional level.
